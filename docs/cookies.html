Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026
In short: this website uses only essential storage to work and to remember your cookie choice. We do not use advertising, marketing, or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not share your browsing with ad networks or social media.

1. What are cookies & local storage?

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser mechanism for saving small pieces of information on your device. Both let a site remember things between visits.

2. What we use, and why

NameTypePurposeRetention
c2f_cookie_consent Strictly necessary (local storage) Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential storage, so we don't ask again. Until you clear it

That is currently the only item this website stores. It is essential to respect your choice, so it is not subject to consent.

3. What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No social-media pixels or share-tracking.
  • No cross-site or cross-app tracking, and no selling of data.

4. The consent banner

Because we currently set only essential storage, our banner is informational and records your preference. If we ever add analytics or advertising cookies, the banner will be updated so that such non-essential cookies load only after you agree, in line with the ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, LGPD, and CCPA/CPRA. You will be able to change your choice at any time.

5. The mobile app

The mobile app does not use website cookies. It stores your session and your activity data on your device using the operating system's local storage, so the app works and keeps you signed in.

6. Managing cookies & storage

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings, and you can clear the app's stored data from your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Clearing essential storage may reset your preferences.

7. Changes

We will update this policy if our use of cookies changes, and revise the "last updated" date above.
